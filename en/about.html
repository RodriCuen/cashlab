CashLab is an independent editorial project about cryptocurrencies, saving and digital finance. We explain how things work, what the risks are and how to protect yourself — in clear language, with real examples and no promises of easy money.

Our approach

  • Explain before recommending. We want you to understand, not to buy.
  • Honest about uncertainty. Prices, products and rules change.
  • Security first. Scams, privacy and key custody get priority.
  • Living content. We review articles and fix errors we find.

How we write

Articles are written for educational purposes, checked against official sources, project documentation and regulators. If you find an outdated or wrong detail, tell us.

Independence and advertising

CashLab may be funded by advertising. Ads don't influence our editorial line and are not recommendations. Any sponsored content or affiliate link will be clearly labeled.
